# 1. Company Profile — Nexora Technologies

**Company:** Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd.  
**Founded:** 2021  
**Headquarters:** Bengaluru, India  
**Industry:** Software, Cloud, Data & AI  
**Employees:** Approximately 180  
**Primary Markets:** India, Southeast Asia, Middle East and selected European clients  

---

## Mission & Vision

- **Mission:** Make modern software, data and AI capabilities practical for growing businesses.
- **Vision:** Become a trusted technology partner for organizations modernizing their operations.
- **Business Model:** Nexora combines recurring SaaS subscriptions with implementation, consulting and managed technology services.

---

## Company Overview

Nexora Technologies is a fictional mid-sized IT company that helps organizations modernize customer operations, internal workflows and data platforms. Its delivery model combines reusable cloud software with implementation teams, allowing customers to start with a standard product and add specialized services when required.

## Leadership Structure

The company is organized into Product & Engineering, Cloud & Data, Customer Success, Sales & Partnerships, People Operations, Finance and Corporate Operations. A five-person executive leadership team oversees strategy, technology, finance and go-to-market execution.
