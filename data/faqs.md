# 6. FAQs — Nexora Technologies

### Q1: Is Nexora a real company?
**A:** No. Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is fictional and is used for demonstration, documentation and project purposes.

---

### Q2: Who are Nexora's typical customers?
**A:** Mid-sized businesses and enterprise teams that need workflow automation, analytics, AI-enabled knowledge tools or cloud modernization.

---

### Q3: Does Nexora offer a free trial?
**A:** Yes. Eligible customers can request a 14-day product trial for selected SaaS plans. Enterprise evaluations are arranged case by case.

---

### Q4: Can products integrate with existing systems?
**A:** Yes. Standard integrations and REST APIs are available, with custom integration services for systems that require additional engineering.

---

### Q5: Where is customer data hosted?
**A:** Hosting location depends on the selected service and contract. Customers can discuss regional hosting, retention and security requirements during procurement.

---

### Q6: Does Nexora provide technical support?
**A:** Yes. Standard support is included with paid SaaS subscriptions. Enterprise plans can include priority support and defined service-level commitments.

---

### Q7: How long does implementation take?
**A:** A standard SaaS rollout can take two to six weeks. More complex integrations and custom projects generally take longer and are scoped during discovery.

---

### Q8: How can someone apply for a job?
**A:** Open positions are published through the company's fictional careers channel in this documentation scenario. Candidates should submit a résumé and role-specific application materials.

---

### Q9: Can customers request custom features?
**A:** Yes. Product requests are evaluated based on customer value, technical feasibility, security and roadmap priorities. Custom development can also be contracted as a professional service.

---

### Q10: How are invoices handled?
**A:** Invoices are issued according to the order form. SaaS subscriptions are generally billed in advance, while professional services follow the agreed milestone or monthly billing schedule.
