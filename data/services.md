# 3. Services — Nexora Technologies

Nexora Technologies delivers professional and managed technology services to help organizations modernize their architecture, build custom applications, and operationalize data and AI.

---

## Service Offerings

| Service | Scope | Typical engagement |
|---|---|---|
| **Cloud Modernization** | Assessment, migration planning, application modernization and cloud operations. | Typical engagement: 6–16 weeks |
| **Data & BI Consulting** | Data modeling, ETL/ELT pipelines, dashboard development and analytics enablement. | Typical engagement: 4–12 weeks |
| **AI Solutions** | AI readiness assessment, retrieval-augmented applications, workflow automation and governance. | Typical engagement: 6–14 weeks |
| **Custom Software Development** | Web and API development, integrations, testing and production support. | Typical engagement: 8–24 weeks |
| **Managed Support** | Monitoring, incident response, platform maintenance and service reviews. | Monthly managed service |

---

## Service Delivery Methodology

Most projects begin with a discovery phase followed by solution design, implementation, testing, knowledge transfer and post-launch support. Customers receive a named delivery lead and regular status reporting for engagements longer than four weeks.
