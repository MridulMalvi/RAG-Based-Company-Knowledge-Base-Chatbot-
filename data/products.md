## Detailed Product Descriptions

### 1. Nexora Flow
- **Overview:** Workflow automation platform for approvals, service requests, internal processes and notifications.
- **Starting Price:** ₹1,499/user/month
- **Primary Audience:** Business teams looking to automate repetitive business processes and streamline team handoffs.

### 2. Nexora Insight
- **Overview:** Cloud analytics platform with dashboards, KPI tracking, scheduled reports and role-based access.
- **Starting Price:** ₹24,999/workspace/month
- **Primary Audience:** Data & leadership teams requiring real-time business visibility and metric tracking.

### 3. Nexora Assist
- **Overview:** Enterprise AI assistant for knowledge search, document Q&A, drafting and controlled internal workflows.
- **Starting Price:** ₹799/user/month
- **Primary Audience:** Knowledge workers seeking fast information retrieval and AI-assisted productivity.

---

## Product Principles

All products use cloud-hosted infrastructure, role-based access controls, audit logging and configurable retention policies. Enterprise plans can include single sign-on, dedicated environments, advanced integrations and custom security requirements.
