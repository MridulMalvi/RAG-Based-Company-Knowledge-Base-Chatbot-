# 5. HR Policies — Nexora Technologies

---

## Working Hours & Flexibility

Standard working hours are 9:30 AM–6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Teams may use flexible schedules when business needs permit. Customer-facing teams may follow client-aligned hours.

---

## Leave Policy

- **Paid Annual Leave:** 18 days per year.
- **Casual / Sick Leave:** 10 days per year.
- **Holidays:** Declared company holidays, subject to applicable law and employment terms.
- **Notice for Planned Leave:** Planned leave should normally be requested at least three working days in advance.

---

## Remote & Hybrid Work

Eligible roles may work remotely for up to three days per week with manager approval. Employees are expected to maintain reliable connectivity, protect company information and attend required meetings.

---

## Performance Reviews

Formal performance reviews occur twice a year, with quarterly check-ins focused on goals, feedback, development and role expectations.

---

## Learning & Development

Employees receive an annual learning allowance of up to ₹15,000 for approved courses, certifications, conferences or professional resources.

---

## Code of Conduct

Employees must act professionally, avoid conflicts of interest, protect confidential information and comply with applicable laws and company policies. Harassment, discrimination, retaliation and workplace violence are prohibited.

---

## Information Security

Company systems require appropriate authentication and access controls. Confidential data must not be copied to personal accounts or unauthorized services. Suspected security incidents must be reported immediately.

---

## Equal Opportunity

Employment decisions are based on role requirements, qualifications, performance and business needs. The company is committed to a respectful workplace free from unlawful discrimination.

---

## Notice & Separation

Notice periods follow the employment contract and applicable law. Company property, credentials and confidential information must be returned or securely transferred when employment ends.
